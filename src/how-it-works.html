How railcheck works

An amount can point to a deposit. An address link can reveal more. railcheck checks these 2 risks.

What railcheck checks

railcheck compares the amount with every RAILGUN ETH deposit of the last 180 days.

The pool is RAILGUN ETH (WETH) on Ethereum. Enter the amount before the 0.25% unshield fee. railcheck does not ask which deposit is yours.

How the check works

railcheck looks for sets of 1, 2 or 3 deposits that add up to your amount.

  1. The check allows a difference of up to 0.000005 ETH. It adds amounts before it compares them.
  2. Each matching set gets a weight. Smaller sets get a higher prior weight. The check also includes the chance of no exact set.
  3. The strongest deposit match sets the amount score. Many competing matches can reduce it. The result table shows matching sets of 1, 2 or 3 deposits (each leg with its amount sent and date), not lone legs that only make sense with partners.
  4. An address match sets a score of at least 100. A past transaction between the addresses sets at least 90.
  5. Withdrawing the rest of a partly withdrawn deposit sets at least 90.
  6. Not sure uses the worse answer. It does not prove that a link exists.

When the amount score is 6 or more, railcheck tries amounts that are 10% to 30% lower. It shows the first amount with a score of 5 or less and at least 20 nearby deposits.

What the score means

The score runs from 0 to 100 and uses the strongest signal.

Score Band Meaning
0–5 Low No strong amount match was found.
6–20 Medium The amount matches a deposit set.
21–50 High The amount gives a stronger link.
51–100 Critical The amount or your address answers give a strong signal.

A score is a model output, not a measured chance that someone will identify you. A deposit match applies only if that deposit is yours.

Privacy

Your amount and answers stay in this tab.

The check does not send them to a server, put them in the URL, or save them on your device. There is no wallet connection. There are no analytics or third-party page resources.

Every visitor gets the same pool file. The worker checks its file hash before it uses the data.

A host can receive your IP address when you load the page. A VPN or Tor can mask your IP address from the host. It does not remove all privacy risks.

Limits

Off-chain data can make the real risk higher.

  • The check covers 180 days. Older deposits and sets of more than 3 deposits are outside this model.
  • The model assumes that a broadcaster sends the withdrawal.
  • If this withdrawal is the rest of a deposit you already partly withdrew, answer Yes to that question. The combined amounts can create a link.
  • A lower amount score does not remove a link between addresses.
  • The score uses fixed model weights. A model can miss a real link.

Questions

Can someone link my RAILGUN withdrawal to my deposit?

Yes. An amount or an address link can connect a withdrawal to a deposit. Other data can also create a link.

Why does the withdrawal amount matter?

An amount can equal 1 deposit or a sum of 2 or 3 deposits. A rare match can point to its source. The result table lists those sets, with each deposit’s amount sent and date.

Does railcheck see my amount or my address?

Your browser sees the amount so it can run the check. The site does not receive it. railcheck never asks for an address.

Which deposits does railcheck compare?

railcheck compares RAILGUN ETH deposits on Ethereum from the last 180 days. It excludes shields in transactions that spend private notes.

What amount should I withdraw?

If the amount points to deposits, railcheck can suggest a lower amount. Check it with your own address answers. A Low score does not remove all risk.

Is railcheck free and open source?

railcheck is free and open source under the MIT license.
