What railcheck does

An unusual amount can show which shield is yours. railcheck compares your shield or unshield amount with the RAILGUN WETH shields on Ethereum from the last 180 days, and gives a risk score before you send. The check runs on your device and never asks for an address. It does not cover timing or IP addresses.
